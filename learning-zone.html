Animals and Humans

⚡ Test in Practice Lab
📐 Core Axiom

Animals, including humans, have basic needs for survival and distinct body structures.

⚠️ Cognitive Trap (Common Error)

Believing that humans are not animals or that all animals have the same dietary needs.

📋 Structured Lesson Steps

Step 1: Inquiry Hook

How do different animals survive in varying environments compared to humans?

Step 2: Guided Practice & Activity

Identify, compare, and classify common animals by their physical structures and diets.

Step 3: Socratic Check for Understanding

"What essential things do all animals need to stay alive?"
