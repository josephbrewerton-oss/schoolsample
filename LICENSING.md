## 3. Privacy & Zero-Telemetry Architecture
This platform is engineered as an offline-first, client-side learning environment:
* **Zero Telemetry:** No pupil profiles, interaction telemetry, voice recordings, or behavioral data are transmitted to external servers or cloud AI providers.
* **Local Processing:** Pedagogical AST evaluations and peer-to-peer data transfers execute client-side or across local network WebRTC DataChannels.
* **Compliance:** Deployments adhere to GDPR and UK Data Protection Act 2018 standards without data processing addendums (DPAs).
